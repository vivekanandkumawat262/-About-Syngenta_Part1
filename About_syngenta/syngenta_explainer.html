Section 1

About Syngenta

Syngenta is one of the world's largest agriculture companies — present in over 100 countries with approximately 60,000 employees helping farmers grow better crops.

🌾
In simple words:

Syngenta is a very big agriculture company that operates around the world. It has thousands of workers and operates in more than 100 countries. Its main mission is to help farmers grow more food safely and sustainably.

Section 2

What Does Syngenta Make?

Syngenta creates two main things: crop protection products and seeds. Crop protection products help farmers fight weeds, fungi, and insects.

🌿

Herbicides

Products that kill unwanted grass and weeds growing around crops, so the crop gets all the water and nutrients.

📌 Example: Weed killer
🍄

Fungicides

Products that protect crops from fungal diseases like mold, rust, and rot that can destroy an entire harvest.

📌 Example: Anti-fungal spray
🦟

Insecticides

Products that kill harmful insects and pests that eat crops, protecting the farmer's hard work from being destroyed.

📌 Example: Pest spray
🌱
They also make Seeds

Special seeds that are designed to help farmers grow more food in a better, more sustainable way — even in difficult weather or soil conditions.

Section 3

Where Does Syngenta Work?

In addition to global operations, Syngenta has a special focus on a region called AMEA — covering four major areas.

Sections 4, 5 & 6

Farmers & Their Challenges

Syngenta's main customers are small farmers — people who own less than 5 acres of land — mostly in rural villages. Selling to them is far harder than selling in cities.

⛅

Weather

Too much rain, drought, extreme heat — different every season and every region.

🪨

Soil Quality

Some areas have poor soil that lacks the nutrients needed for crops to grow well.

🐛

Pest Pressure

Insects and diseases attack crops differently in each region, requiring local solutions.

📉

Market Conditions

Crop prices keep changing. Low prices after a good harvest can still leave farmers in losses.

⚠️ Key insight: Farmers live in different villages, speak many languages, grow different crops, and face different local problems. One solution does not work for all.

Section 7

Who Connects Syngenta with Farmers?

Thousands of field workers bridge the gap between Syngenta's laboratories and the farmers working in their fields every day.

🧑‍💼

Sales Representatives

Visit farmers directly, demonstrate products, and ensure the right solutions reach the right farmer at the right time.

🧑‍🔬

Agronomists

Agricultural scientists and experts who guide farmers on best practices, crop health, and how to use products correctly.
